## 🎮 What is this game?

The hack-day-swap-game is a puzzle project for your web browser. You match colors to clear blocks from the screen. The game challenges you to move tokens into a memory tray. Your goal is to fill every socket to repair the robot. 

This game works as a browser-based application. You do not need to install complex software to play. It functions within your standard internet browser. This keeps your computer clean and makes starting the game simple.

## 🕹️ How to Play

The game mechanics involve matching colors and moving tokens. Here is how you control the game:

- **Selecting Clusters:** Use your mouse to click on groups of identical colors. If you click a group of three or more, they clear from the board. 
- **The Memory Tray:** When you clear tokens, some items move into the memory tray. You manage this tray to store specific pieces for later use.
- **Filling Sockets:** Your main objective involves the robot repair area. You must place the correct tokens into the empty sockets. 
- **Strategy:** Plan your moves to maximize your points. If you clear many tokens at once, you earn a multiplier. Watch the robot's status bar to track your progress.

The game tracks your score automatically. You can pause the game at any time if you need a break.
